Browse the full colour library. No screen shows paint exactly, so get a tester before you buy. Colour values are each brand's own published digital swatches, except Zoffany, Sanderson and Fired Earth, which publish no values, so they are read from the swatch images on their own sites (Zoffany, Sanderson, Fired Earth). Colour names are the property of their respective brands. Every colour page links straight to that colour: the brand's own page for each colour for most brands; Valspar to the colour's tester pot at B&Q, or Valspar's own page for the few B&Q doesn't list; Lick to the colour with its sample selected; Lakeland Paints to its tester page, where you pick the colour, as the brand has no page per colour.
